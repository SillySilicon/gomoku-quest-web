About Forbidden Moves

In Renju, to prevent Black from having too much advantage, there are three forbidden moves (double three, double four, overline). White has no forbidden moves.

Double three: Creating two or more open threes at the same time.

In the diagram below, Black cannot place a stone at the ✖️ marks because it would create a double three.

The strict definition of a three will be explained later, but in the diagram below, the threes are blocked by White stones, so none of them are double threes.

Double four: Creating two or more open fours at the same time.

Overline: Placing six or more stones in a row.

These are the basic rules. In the next chapter, we will explain basic strategies.
