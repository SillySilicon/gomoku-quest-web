In the Renju rules explanation, we mentioned that Black has forbidden moves: "double three", "double four", and "overlines".
This means that, aside from cases where White fails to block a three, Black's only way to win is by making a four-three. In contrast, White has various ways to win.
In this chapter, we will explain the most "White-like" way to win: winning by forcing Black to make a forbidden move.

First, look at the diagram below. For Black, the point marked with ✖️ is a forbidden double three. Therefore, White will try to force Black to play there.
Try pressing the arrow buttons. White makes a broken three. Even if Black blocks the outside, White then makes a four, and it's game over. The only way for black to stop this is to play on the forbidden point.

In actual games, a double three forbidden point often appears early in the game.
The diagram below shows a common double three trap. White guides the game so that the ✖️ point becomes a double three for Black.

Summary

Winning by forcing a forbidden move is a classic White strategy, and it's very satisfying when you pull it off.
When you play as White in a real game, try to always be conscious of winning by forcing a forbidden move.
