In this chapter, we will explain the "Knight's Net", a hurdle that everyone faces in Gomoku Quest. The first time you encounter this, it can feel hopeless, so please learn the counter-strategy here.

First, play through the game in the diagram below using the arrows.
Did you notice how the white stones are being placed in a regular pattern, surrounding the black stones?

If we extract only the White stones, it looks like the diagram below. This formation is called the "Knight's Net." It's beautiful, isn't it?
Inside the Knight's Net, there is only a 4-space gap both vertically and horizontally, making it impossible to form a five-in-a-row. Just like Black in the previous example, no matter how many threes or fours you make inside the net, you will only get cornered by White and find yourself in a tougher position.
