What is Renju?

The rules of Gomoku Quest are based on Renju.

Renju is a refined version of Gomoku that ensures fairness for both players. The objective of the game is to line up five stones before your opponent.

The first move must be at the center of the board, the second move must be adjacent to the center, and the third move must be within the central 5x5 area. After that, you can play anywhere.
