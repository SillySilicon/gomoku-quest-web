A four is a shape that will become a five-in-a-row if one more stone is placed.

Just like threes, you should not waste your fours recklessly. However, winning with a series of fours is called VCF (Victory by Continuous Fours). If you have a VCF, you can achieve victory in one go without needing to block the opponent's three.

In the diagram below, White has a three, but Black can win with VCF without blocking it. Try pressing the arrow buttons.

VCF is a very important technique in Renju. In Gomoku Quest's "Study", there are "VCF Puzzles" and "VCF Challenges", so please make use of them.
